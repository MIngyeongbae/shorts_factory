# The Brooklyn Bridge — why the bad wire in its cables is still in there

- 시드: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_Bridge
- 매체 적합성: Strong — the bundled wires in a cable cross-section, the pass/fail marks from inspection, a bar dropping from 6–8 times safety down to 4, the 150 wires added on top: the pictures carry the understanding
- 핵심 질문: Why wasn't a bridge built with faulty wire torn down, and how is it still standing 143 years later?
- 핵심 반전: The designer overbuilt to six or eight times the strength needed, and that margin absorbed exactly what the fraud ate away
- 보이는 것: Thousands of wires being spun and set into a cable, bad wire slipping in among them, the safety multiple falling, wire added after the fact
- 차별점: The cleanup — the swindler was never fired, he only paid for the replacement wire — and the fact that his bad wire is still inside the bridge today
