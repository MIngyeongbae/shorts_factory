# The moment it's in your hand, the mug is worth double

- 시드: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endowment_effect
- 매체 적합성: Fits — two different price tags on one identical cup, a hand gripping the mug against an empty hand, the layout of the "second mug" experiment; the visuals carry the argument instead of the words
- 핵심 질문: Why does the price a seller asks for a mug come out double what a buyer will pay for the very same mug?
- 핵심 반전: The usual explanation is "people hate losing things," but people with nothing to lose — owners already holding a mug, asked what they'd pay for a second one — named just as high a price. The culprit looks like ownership, not loss
- 보이는 것: two price tags on one cup, the value jumping the moment it lands in a hand, a second mug held out to an owner, free-trial and free-return buttons
- 차별점: the layer underneath that popular books rarely go past — the famous reading of this experiment (loss aversion) was challenged by a follow-up study
- 여운: The things in your room are inflated about twofold too

## 대본

Why is your own stuff always worth more to you than to anyone else?
Kahneman's team handed mugs to half the students at Cornell.
The half holding a mug were asked what they'd sell it for.
The half without one were asked what they'd pay for it.
Here's the result. Sellers wanted nearly twice what buyers offered.
With Final Four basketball tickets, the gap was 14 times.
Same object. One thing changed — whether it's in your hand right now.
The first explanation went like this. Losing hurts more than gaining feels good.
But then a rather strange follow-up experiment showed up.
They asked people who already had a mug what they'd pay for a second one.
This person has nothing to lose. They still named a high price.
The culprit seems to be ownership itself, not loss.
The moment it's yours, the theory goes, a mug stops being an object and describes you.
Meanwhile, the seller's mind reaches first for reasons to keep the mug,
and the buyer's reaches, instead, for reasons to keep the money.
That's why free trials and free returns are everywhere.
Because the moment you try it, it's already yours.
The mug's price tag was never on the mug. It was on the palm holding it.

## 주장

- [줄 1] Sellers' prices (WTA) run higher than buyers' (WTP) even for items with no cause for attachment, or obtained only minutes ago — 확인 (Endowment effect 도입부)
- [줄 2] Daniel Kahneman, Jack Knetsch and Richard Thaler ran an experiment handing mugs to Cornell undergraduates (1990) — 확인 (Examples 절 / KKT 1990 JPE 98:1325)
- [줄 2] The mugs went to half the participants — alternating students — not to everyone (mug valued at about $6) — 확인 (KKT 1990)
- [줄 3] Participants who now owned a mug were asked what they would accept to sell it (willingness to accept) — 확인 (Examples 절)
- [줄 4] Participants who received no mug were asked what they would pay for one (willingness to pay) — 확인 (Examples 절 / 도입부)
- [줄 5] Owners' asking price was roughly twice non-owners' willingness to pay (median $5.25 vs $2.25–2.75) — 확인 (Kahneman, Knetsch & Thaler 1990)
- [줄 6] In Ziv Carmon and Dan Ariely's study, hypothetical selling prices for NCAA Final Four tickets ran about 14 times hypothetical buying prices (median $2,411 vs $175) — 확인 (Carmon & Ariely 2000)
- [줄 7] The only difference between the two groups is ownership, not the object (random assignment) — 확인 (도입부 valuation paradigm)
- [줄 8] Loss aversion is the first and leading explanation for the gap, grounded in prospect theory's claim that losses loom larger than equivalent gains — 확인 (Loss aversion 절)
- [줄 9~10] Morewedge, Shu, Gilbert & Wilson (2009) asked participants who had been given one mug what they would pay for a second — 확인 (Connection-based 절 / JESP 원문)
- [줄 11] These "owner-buyers" valued the mug as highly as "owner-sellers" despite not being in a selling position, and higher than buyers who got no mug — 확인 (같은 절)
- [줄 12] Ownership, not loss aversion, is the cause — 유력설 (손실 회피가 여전히 선도 설명이고 심리적 관성·기준 가격·질의 이론 등 경쟁 가설 존재) → 줄 12는 이미 추정 표현, 유지
- [줄 13] Attachment theory: an owned object is absorbed into the owner's self-concept and becomes part of their identity — 유력설 (여러 경쟁 이론 중 하나) → "바뀐다는 거죠"로 완화
- [줄 14~15] Johnson, Häubl & Keinan (2007): prospective buyers recalled reasons to keep their money first, sellers recalled reasons to keep the mug first — 확인 (Biased information processing 절)
- [줄 14] Causal claim that this recall order follows from the "ownership = self" theory — 근거 없음 (질의 이론은 소유가 아닌 질의 순서를 원인으로 보는 별개 설명) → 연결어 "그래서" → "한편"
- [줄 16~17] Free trials and free returns are used in marketing to create a sense of ownership and cut cancellations and returns — 확인 (Implications > Business)
