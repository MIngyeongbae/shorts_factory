# Hay fever — Japan's national illness was planted by policy, not by nature

- 시드: https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/花粉症
- 매체 적합성: Suitable — broadleaf forest flipping into cedar plantation, a pollen-output curve that bends upward at tree age 30, and a prevalence line climbing from 16% in 1998 to 39% in 2019 draw the causal chain on screen.
- 핵심 질문: Why is it only in Japan that four in ten people come down with the same allergy every spring?
- 핵심 반전: The cause isn't nature, it's timber policy. The trees were planted to be cut, cheap imports meant nobody cut them, and cedars past 30 years old entered their breeding stage and started pouring pollen.
- 보이는 것: A forest that was nine-tenths broadleaf → plantations that are 97% conifer, pollen output spiking at tree age 30, prevalence rising from 16% to 39%.
- 차별점: A "planted but never cut" decision that came back thirty years later as a national illness — a causal chain unique to Japan's hay fever.

## 대본

Four in ten Japanese people get it every spring. What if policy planted it?
This is the story of hay fever — an allergy to cedar pollen.
In 1948, a survey found ragweed and grass pollen weren't the problem.
Then came the boom years, and construction sent timber demand soaring.
Fast-growing, straight-trunked cedar and cypress went in nationwide.
Forests were said to be nine-tenths broadleaf; plantations are 97% conifer.
The plan was to let them grow, cut them, and use them as house beams.
But cheap imported timber arrived, and nobody cut anything down.
Those planted cedars, it seems, were a 30-year timer.
Past 30 years old, a cedar shifts into making offspring.
From that point on, it starts pouring pollen into the sky.
By the mid-1970s patients spiked — three to four times as many.
In 1976, pollen buried the Kanto region and it became a public issue.
Once your body files pollen as an enemy, it never deletes the file.
Only one or two in ten outgrow it, so the patients kept stacking up.
Prevalence was 16% in 1998. By 2019, it was 39%.
Policy planted this national illness. Now policy is cutting it down.

## 주장

- [줄 1] Per the 2019 national epidemiological survey, hay fever prevalence is 42.5% (cedar hay fever 38.8%) — about four in ten people — 확인 · https://www.env.go.jp/chemi/anzen/kafun/2022_full.pdf ("half the country" is Tokyo's FY2016 figure of 48.8% for cedar hay fever, not a national number · https://www.tmiph.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/files/kj_kankyo/kafun/jittai/houkokusho.pdf)
- [줄 2] About 80% of hay fever cases are cedar hay fever, called a "new national illness" — 확인 · 시드 「歴史 > 日本史」절
- [줄 3] A 1948 survey by an occupation-force medical officer concluded that ragweed and grass pollen were not important allergens in Japan — 확인 · 시드 「歴史 > 日本史」절 (limited to ragweed and grasses)
- [줄 4] Rising demand for construction timber during rapid economic growth was the motive for planting — 확인 · 시드 「環境要因」절, FY2023 Forest and Forestry White Paper
- [줄 5] Fast-growing, easily worked, straight-trunked conifers such as cedar and cypress were planted across the country — 확인 · 같은 절
- [줄 6a] Nearly 90% of Japan's original forest was broadleaf — 유력설 · 시드 서술에 출처 각주 없음
- [줄 6b] Conifers make up about 97% of today's plantations (broadleaf 3%) — 정정 · https://www.rinya.maff.go.jp/j/kikaku/hakusyo/r1hakusyo_h/all/chap1_1_1.html (시드의 "99%"는 공식 통계와 어긋남)
- [줄 7] The purpose of the planting was construction timber — 확인 · 같은 절
- [줄 8] The end of rapid growth and the influx of cheap imported timber left Japan's conifer forests untended — 확인 · 같은 절
- [줄 9] The roughly 30-year lag between planting and the pollen surge is an inference the source hedges as "thought to be the reason" — 유력설 · 같은 절 (대본은 "it seems"로 추정 표기)
- [줄 10] Cedar and cypress shift into a reproductive stage past tree age 30 — 확인 · 같은 절 (단 林野庁은 "20년생 이후 비산량 증가"로 서술 · https://www.rinya.maff.go.jp/j/sin_riyou/kafun/qanda.html)
- [줄 11] At that stage they release especially large amounts of pollen, and the untended conifer forests began emitting it in bulk — 확인 · 같은 절
- [줄 12] Cedar hay fever patients surged from the mid-1970s, and clinic-visit trends support reports of a three- to fourfold increase during the 1970s — 확인 · 시드 「歴史 > 日本史」절, 「疫学 > 患者数」절
- [줄 13] The first mass dispersal hit the Kanto region in 1976, and it was around this period (including the 1979 and 1982 dispersals) that it was recognized as a social problem — 확인 · 시드 「歴史 > 日本史」절 (전국적 현상은 아니었음)
- [줄 14] Once sensitization is established, the information "pollen is foreign" is remembered, and spontaneous recovery is in principle difficult — 확인 · 시드 「発症まで」절
- [줄 15] There is no established figure for the spontaneous recovery rate, but it is said to be roughly one or two in ten, so patient numbers accumulate year over year — 유력설 · 시드 「疫学 > 自然治癒率」절 (각주 없음)
- [줄 16] National prevalence of cedar hay fever rose from 16% in 1998 to 39% in 2019 — about 10 points per decade — 확인 · https://www.rinya.maff.go.jp/j/kikaku/hakusyo/r5hakusyo_h/summary/s_tokusyu.html (시드의 "1980년대 1할 미만 → 2016년 약 5할"은 각주가 개인 클리닉 칼럼)
- [줄 17] On October 11, 2023 the Japanese government adopted an "Initial Intensive Response Package for Hay Fever," accelerating the felling and replanting of cedar plantations at the source (target: cedar plantations down about 20% by FY2033, pollen output halved in roughly 30 years) — 확인 · https://www.cas.go.jp/jp/seisaku/kafun/pdf/231011_honbun.pdf , https://www.rinya.maff.go.jp/j/sin_riyou/kafun/qanda.html
