## 핵심 발췌

> Ten arc seconds are added to the tilt after boring both into the masonry foundations and accidentally into the soil beneath them.

> Between May 1993 and January 1994, crews lay down a series of specially cast lead ingots. By July 1994, the tower has leaned back toward the north...a full 52 arc seconds.

> As soon as the freezing stops, however, the tower begins to lean south at a rate of four arc seconds per day.

> In December engineers install temporary cables, which can be tensioned to steady the tower.

> By the end of August the lean had decreased by 130 arc seconds (1.5 inches).
