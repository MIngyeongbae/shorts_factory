## 핵심 발췌

> Artificial cooling of concrete by embedded pipe was first tested on the Owyhee Arch Dam Project in the United States.

> China first used artificial cooling technology-embedded cooling water pipes in an arch dam, that is, the XiangHongDian project, in 1955.
