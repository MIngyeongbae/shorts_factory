## 핵심 발췌

> Kuwait and Qatar, where the full crude capacities are stacked without any possibility to go around the vulnerable strait.

> the total volumes are far from enough to offset the entire drop from the crisis-hit strait

> Only Saudi Arabia is currently capable of channelling its entire crude oil exports without passing through the strait.
