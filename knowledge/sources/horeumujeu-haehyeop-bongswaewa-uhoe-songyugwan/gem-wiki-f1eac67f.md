## 핵심 발췌

> The pipeline extending 1568km from Al-Zubair in Iraq to Yanbu in Saudi Arabia

> 1,650,000 barrels per day

> $2.25 billion

> in order to diversify export routes after oil tankers were attacked in the Gulf during the Iran-Iraq war

> out of operation since 1990

> Saudi Arabia confiscated the pipeline in 2001 as compensation for debts owed by Baghdad
