## 핵심 발췌

> The pipeline does not solve the chokepoint problem; it merely relocates it.

> Every tanker leaving Saudi Arabia's western Yanbu Port for Asia must transit Bab al-Mandeb, leaving an estimated 70–75 percent of Yanbu's exports directly exposed to Houthi disruption.

> The alternative route, via the Cape of Good Hope, adds 10–14 days and US$1.2-1.8 million in additional fuel costs per round trip.
