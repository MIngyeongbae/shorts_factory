## 핵심 발췌

> 1,201-kilometre-long (746 mi) pipeline in Saudi Arabia that runs from the Abqaiq oil field in the Eastern Province...to Yanbu at the Red Sea

> two pipes, one 142 centimetres (56 in) in diameter and the other...122 centimetres (48 in) in diameter

> amid fears that the Iran-Iraq war would cut off shipping through the Strait of Hormuz

> 2019 Afif attack by Houthi drones targeted the pipeline in May 2019, temporarily shutting it down
