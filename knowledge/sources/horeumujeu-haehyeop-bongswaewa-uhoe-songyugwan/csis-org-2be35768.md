## 핵심 발췌

> Qatar is among the countries most exposed to prolonged disruption in the Strait of Hormuz. Its vast LNG expansion program to nearly double its existing export capacity of ~77 million metric tons per annum (mtpa)

> Unlike oil producers, Qatar has few realistic options for bypassing Hormuz.

> Qatar was able to export approximately 40 loaded LNG tankers through the strait, equivalent to roughly 2.8 million tons
