## 핵심 발췌

> The 1,648-km steel Tapline

> It took three years to complete the Tapline's installation at a cost of USD230 million

> More than sixteen thousand engineers and workers took part in its construction

> more than 200,000 nine-meter long steel pipes

> Jordan and Syria, and ending in Sidon Port in Lebanon

> The Tapline was affected by the Lebanese Civil War in 1975, thus preventing oil pumping to Sidon in 1983

> Aramco to stop its operation in 1984, and it was eventually decommissioned

> the first industrial heritage site in the Kingdom
