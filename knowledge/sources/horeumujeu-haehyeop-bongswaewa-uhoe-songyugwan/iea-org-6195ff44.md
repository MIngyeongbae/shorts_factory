## 핵심 발췌

> 20 mb/d, around 25% world seaborne oil trade, transits the Strait

> nearly 15 mb/d of crude oil, nearly 34% of global crude oil trade, passed through the Strait of Hormuz

> 3.5 to 5.5 mb/d of available capacity to export crude out of the Gulf on alternative routes

> about 2 mb/d of the pipeline's capacity is used, leaving between 3 and 5 mb/d of spare capacity

> 1.1 mb/d of domestic crude via this route, leaving room for up to 700 kb/d of additional volumes

> The pipeline and port effectively remain non-operational

> currently not considered a viable crude export option

> almost 112 bcm in 2025, equating to almost 20% of global LNG trade
