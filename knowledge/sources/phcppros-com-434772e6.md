## 핵심 발췌

> Six hundred miles of steel pipe was installed through the different layers of concrete pours.

> the steel pipe was filled with grout, pumped in at 300 psi

> a maximum of 1,000 tons of ice per day

> It was the largest refrigerator in the world at the time.

> a crane would deliver an enormous bucket of concrete every 78 seconds
