## 핵심 발췌

> There are absolutely no bodies buried in the Hoover Dam. Ever.

> The forms were big enough that the concrete, that pore, would only come up maybe to about a worker's ankles.

> It would create a kind of bubble inside the structure, certainly compromising it.

> The body's decomposition doesn't happen in the time it takes for the concrete to set. The concrete just would not have been able to set properly.
