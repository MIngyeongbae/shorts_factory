## 핵심 발췌

> the Owyhee River...11 miles southwest of Adrian, Oregon

> Owyhee Dam, the tallest in the world when completed in 1932

> Bureau engineers experimented at Owyhee by funneling artificially cooled water through a network of one-inch coils imbedded in concrete panels.

> The cooling system subsequently used at Hoover Dam was only slightly altered from that used at Owyhee.

> Construction began in January 1928

> Pouring of mass concrete was completed on May 28, 1932
