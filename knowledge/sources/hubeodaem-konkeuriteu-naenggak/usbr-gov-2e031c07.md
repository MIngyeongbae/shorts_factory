## 핵심 발췌

> 582 miles of 1-inch steel pipe

> 1,000 tons of ice in 24 hours

> Cooling was completed in March 1935.

> 726.4 feet from foundation rock to the roadway on the crest of the dam.

> More than 6,600,000 tons.

> 21,000 men worked on the dam with an average of 3,500 and a maximum of 5,218 daily.

> 4,360,000 cubic yards of concrete in the dam, powerplant and appurtenant works
