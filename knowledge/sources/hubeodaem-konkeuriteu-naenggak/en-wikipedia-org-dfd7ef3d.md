## 핵심 발췌

> The ground where the dam would rise was marked with rectangles, and concrete blocks in columns were poured, some as large as 50 ft square (15 m) and 5 feet (1.5 m) high.

> Each five-foot form contained a set of 1-inch (25 mm) steel pipes; cool river water would be poured through the pipes, followed by ice-cold water from a refrigeration plant.

> More than 582 miles (937 km) of cooling pipes were placed within the concrete.

> A total of 3,250,000 cubic yards (2,480,000 cubic meters) of concrete was used in the dam before concrete pouring ceased on May 29, 1935.

> The first concrete was poured into the dam on June 6, 1933, 18 months ahead of schedule.

> Grout was also used to fill the hairline spaces between columns, which were grooved to increase the strength of the joints.
