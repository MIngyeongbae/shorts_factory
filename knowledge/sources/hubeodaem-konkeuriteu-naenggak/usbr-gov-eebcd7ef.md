## 핵심 발췌

> Guided Dam Tour: $40 per person / Children 3 and under: free

> Walk through the inspection tunnels at the center of Hoover Dam

> View the Colorado River through the inspection ventilation shaft

> Visit the viewing platform overlooking a 30 foot diameter penstock

> Open Daily 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. (except Thanksgiving and Christmas Day)

> Open to the public daily from 5:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. (PST)
