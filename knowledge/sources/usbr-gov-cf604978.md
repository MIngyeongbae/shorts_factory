## 핵심 발췌

> Bureau of Reclamation engineers calculated that if the dam were built in a single continuous pour, the concrete would have gotten so hot that it would have taken 125 years for the concrete to cool to ambient temperatures.

> Rather than being a single block of concrete, the dam was built as a series of individual columns

> Each form also contained cooling coils of 1" thin-walled steel pipe.

> chilled water from a refrigeration plant on the lower cofferdam

> Grout was forced into these joints, bonding the entire structure into a monolithic whole.

> The first concrete was placed into the dam on June 6, 1933.

> More than 5 million barrels of Portland cement and 4.5 million cubic yards of aggregate went into the dam.
