## 핵심 발췌

> opened to the general public on March 28, 2007

> The Skywalk length extending out from the post supports closest to the canyon wall is 70 feet (21 m)

> The vertical drop directly below the skywalk is 500 to 800 feet (150 to 240 m)

> The walkway could carry 822 people that weigh 200 pounds (91 kg) each without overstress, but maximum occupancy at one time is 120 people

> In 2015, the attraction drew a million visitors in a year for the first time
