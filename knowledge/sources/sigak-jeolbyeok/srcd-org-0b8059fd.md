## 핵심 발췌

> attempted to walk down impossibly large drop-offs, even the 90-cm cliff

> tried to crawl down safe drop-offs within their abilities and refused to crawl down drop-offs that were too large

> didn't try to walk down risky drop-offs, but did go down using alternative strategies

> These results suggest that the classic explanation for why infants come to avoid a dropoff—fear of heights—is incorrect.
