## 핵심 발췌

> the invention of the visual cliff was the serendipitous result of Gibson's and Walk's attempt to get more use out of painstakingly dark-reared rats

> this research came to a premature end when she returned to the farm one weekend only to discover that some of her subjects had been given away as Easter presents

> this meant that Eleanor would work as an unpaid research associate at Cornell for 16 years. From 1949–1966, Gibson carried out her research by applying for government grants and partnering with Cornell faculty

> Their findings were published in Scientific American and covered in the popular press, including a feature in Life magazine

> she received many accolades for her work, including the National Medal of Science in 1992. She was only the fifth psychologist ever to receive the award. Gibson accepted the award, wryly noting that the medal "has a picture of a man, of course."

> But a woman who would be a scientist needs a lab, too, a lab of her own.
