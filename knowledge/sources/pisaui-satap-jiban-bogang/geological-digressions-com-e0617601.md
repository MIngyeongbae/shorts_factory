## 핵심 발췌

> soil extraction tubes...drilled obliquely (20-30o) to a point just below the north-side foundations

> 12 holes; the tilt angle of the tower was reduced by 0.02 degrees

> a further 41 extraction holes were drilled. Over a period of about 30 months the tilt angle decreased by 0.5 degrees

> By 2008 the total reduction in tilt from both underexcavation and groundwater drainage was about 0.54 degrees
