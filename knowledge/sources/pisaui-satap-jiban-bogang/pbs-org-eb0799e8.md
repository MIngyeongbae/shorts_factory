## 핵심 발췌

> Initially the tower leans to the north 0.2 degrees off of vertical, but by 1278...the tower tilts to the south about one degree, or roughly 2.7 feet

> six steps from the seventh cornice up to the bell chamber's floor on the south side, while only adding four steps on the north side

> digs out a walkway called the catino around the base of the tower, to make the formerly buried foundation steps and column plinths visible

> Gherardesca's 1838 excavation led to a serious increase in inclination...a possible half a degree increase in the lean

> drill 361 holes into the masonry foundation and inject 80 tons of grout...the result is a sudden increase in the tower's tilt of 31 arc seconds
