## 핵심 발췌

> twelve bore holes lined with 220 mm diameter casings

> On 9th February 1999, in an atmosphere of great tension, the first soil extraction took place

> When the northward rotation had reached about 80 arc seconds by early June 1999 the preliminary soil extraction was stopped

> Continuing its rotation northward at a reduced rate until mid September 1999, achieving a reduction of inclination of 132 seconds

> The southern edge of the foundation rose during soil extraction

> Full underexcavation commenced on 21st February 2000

> On 16th January 2001 the last lead ingot was removed from the post-tensioned concrete ring
