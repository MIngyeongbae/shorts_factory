## 핵심 발췌

> over two billion data points with a precision on the order of 3-4 mm, and in over seventy different positions in the building

> eleven years earlier, almost to the day, Stephen Murray...and Peter Allen...produced the first scan of the cathedral—in fact, the first scan of any Gothic cathedral

> the data proved to be so difficult to assemble that it was nearly impossible to use
