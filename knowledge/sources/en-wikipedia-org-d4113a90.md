## 핵심 발췌

> The tower began to sink after construction had progressed to the second floor in 1178. This was due to a mere three-metre foundation, set in weak, unstable subsoil.

> Starting in 1993, 870 tonnes of lead counterweights were added, which straightened the tower slightly.

> 38 cubic metres of soil were removed from underneath the raised end. The tower's tilt was reduced by 45 centimetres, returning to its 1838 position. In total, 70 metric tons of soil were removed.

> In May 2008, engineers announced that the tower had been stabilized such that it had stopped moving for the first time in its history.
