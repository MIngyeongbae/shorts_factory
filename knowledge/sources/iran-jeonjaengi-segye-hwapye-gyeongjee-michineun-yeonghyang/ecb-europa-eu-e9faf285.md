## 핵심 발췌

> The war in the Middle East has made the outlook significantly more uncertain, creating upside risks for inflation and downside risks for economic growth.

> It will have a material impact on near-term inflation through higher energy prices.

> Inflation has been revised up compared with the December projections, especially for 2026. This is because energy prices will be higher owing to the war.

> The scenario analysis suggests that a prolonged disruption in the supply of oil and gas would result in inflation being above, and growth being below, the baseline projections.
