## 핵심 발췌

> cumulative oil supply losses from producers in the Middle East now exceed 1.3 billion barrels

> from around 20 million barrels per day prior to the conflict to an average of 2.7 million barrels per day in March, April and May

> North Sea Dated international crude oil benchmark reached an all-time high of $144 per barrel

> global oil demand will drop by almost 5 million barrels per day in the second quarter of 2026 year-on-year

> In May, the collective action was bringing 2.5 million barrels per day of additional oil to market

> from 2 million barrels per day before the outbreak of the war to more than 5 million barrels per day in early June
