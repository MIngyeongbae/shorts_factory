## 핵심 발췌

> broadly steady at around $5,000 an ounce

> Traders may be anticipating that the US Federal Reserve will halt interest rate cuts and perhaps even raise rates in response to rising inflation.

> Because the dollar has strengthened, and because gold is traded in dollars, it may be harder for interested investors to push the price up.

> Higher oil prices probably will lead to higher inflation, which also will make the dollar more attractive.
