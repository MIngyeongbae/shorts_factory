## 핵심 발췌

> global merchandise trade growth would slow to 1.9% in 2026 from 4.6% in 2025

> Global GDP growth is projected to moderate slightly from 2.9% in 2025 to 2.8% in both 2026 and 2027.

> a scenario where both crude oil and liquefied natural gas (LNG) prices remain elevated throughout 2026 would shave 0.3 percentage points off the GDP forecast for 2026; this would in turn slash 0.5 percentage points off the trade forecast for this year
