## 핵심 발췌

> value of the US dollar rose in the war's first days

> biggest crash since the 2008 financial crisis, dropping up to 12%

> 17% reduction in Qatar's LNG production capacity

> 3–5 years to fully repair

> $270bn, or 90% of the estimated Iranian GDP in 2026

> 70% of their food is imported through the Strait of Hormuz

> at least 10 million barrels per day as of 12 March
