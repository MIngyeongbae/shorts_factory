## 핵심 발췌

> The world price of oil had peaked in 1980 at over US$35 per barrel

> it fell in 1986 from $27 to below $10

> an average of $78.2 in 1981 to an average of $26.8 per barrel in 1986

> Iraq had fought a long and costly war against Iran and had particularly weak revenues.

> In 1985, the Saudis tired of this behavior and decided to punish the undisciplined OPEC countries. The Saudis abandoned their role as swing producer and began producing at full capacity
